Information We Collect

When you use the Game, we may collect the following types of information:

1. Gameplay Data

We collect anonymous gameplay statistics including scores, levels completed, and game settings. This data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and may be synced to Firebase for leaderboard and daily challenge functionality. This data is not personally identifiable.

2. Device Information

We automatically collect certain information about your device, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers (for purchase validation)
  • IP address

3. Purchase Data

If you make in-app purchases, RevenueCat processes your transactions. We do not store your payment information. RevenueCat handles all payment processing and provides us with anonymized purchase validation data.

4. Advertising Data

The free version of the Game may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks. These networks may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • General location (country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the Game
  • Process in-app purchases and validate entitlements
  • Display leaderboards and daily challenges
  • Improve game performance and user experience
  • Detect and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations
