1. Overview

Tetris Architect ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a single-player offline puzzle game developed by Wispers of the Past. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains what data is collected, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can request its deletion.

Tetris Architect does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location, and stores your game progress locally on your device.

4. Advertising & AdMob

Tetris Architect displays rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to deliver personalized ads. EU users are presented with a Google UMP consent form on first launch and may choose between personalized or non-personalized ads.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time:
Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"

5. In-App Purchases

Tetris Architect offers optional in-app purchases (Season Pass — lifetime, yearly, monthly). All purchases are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing and managed by RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment information. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time via your Google Play account.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Tetris Architect is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us so we can take action.

7. EU Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access the data collected about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (see Section 9)
  • Withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

The legal basis for processing your data is your consent (collected via the in-app UMP consent form on first launch) and the legitimate interest of providing the app's core functionality.

8. California Users (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" of personal information. Tetris Architect does not sell personal information. To exercise any CCPA rights, contact us using the details in Section 12.

9. How to Delete Your Data

You have full control over your data. Tetris Architect stores progress only on your device, so deletion is immediate and complete.

Method 1 — Delete in-app (recommended)

  1. Open Tetris Architect
  2. Tap the Settings (⚙️) icon
  3. Tap "Reset all game data"
  4. Confirm — all progress, scores, and preferences are erased instantly

Method 2 — Clear app data system-wide

  1. Android Settings → Apps → Tetris Architect → Storage
  2. Tap Clear data and Clear cache

Method 3 — Uninstall

Uninstalling the app removes 100% of locally stored data from your device.

Method 4 — Advertising ID

To delete the Advertising ID used by AdMob: Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"

Method 5 — Purchase history

Manage subscriptions and purchase history through your Google Play account at play.google.com/store/account.

Need help? Submit a manual deletion request

If you would like us to confirm deletion or have additional questions, email us:

📧 Email Deletion Request

We will respond within 30 days as required by GDPR and CCPA.

10. Security

All data transmitted to third parties (Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Google Play Billing) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS. Local game data is stored in Android's secure app sandbox, accessible only to Tetris Architect.
