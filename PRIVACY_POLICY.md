# Privacy Policy — Whisper Ball

**Effective Date:** February 2026
**Last Updated:** February 2026
**Publisher:** Wispers of the Past
**Application:** Whisper Ball (Android, iOS)

This Privacy Policy explains how Whisper Ball ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the application.

---

## 1. The Short Version

**Whisper Ball does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.**

The app runs entirely on your device. There are no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no tracking tools, and no servers we own that receive your data.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect any of the following:

- Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address, government IDs)
- Contacts, calendar, photos, files, or messages
- Precise or coarse location
- Device sensors beyond what is required to play the game (accelerometer is not used)
- Camera, microphone, or biometric data
- Browser or app history outside Whisper Ball
- Financial, payment, or purchase information
- Health, fitness, or sensitive personal data
- Advertising identifiers (AAID, IDFA)

---

## 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

For the app to function correctly, Whisper Ball saves a small amount of progress data to your device using your operating system's local storage (Android: SharedPreferences via React Native AsyncStorage; iOS: UserDefaults). This data **never leaves your device**.

| Stored on device | Why |
|---|---|
| Campaign level stars (0–3 per level) | Track your progress through the 50-level campaign |
| Best rally count | Display your personal record on the main menu |
| 1972 theme unlock flag | Remember whether you've earned the retro theme |
| Theme / sound / haptics preferences | Restore your chosen settings between sessions |
| Daily Challenge best score by date | Show your daily progress |

You can erase all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device's system settings, or by uninstalling the app.

---

## 4. Permissions

Whisper Ball requests the following Android permission:

- **VIBRATE** — to provide tactile feedback when you hit the ball, bounce off a wall, or score a point. Vibration can be disabled in the in-app Settings screen.

The app does **not** request any other Android or iOS permissions. There is no internet permission required — the app does not access the network.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

Whisper Ball does **not** integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, social, or backend services. There are no third-party SDKs in the production build that collect user data.

Audio files included with the app are bundled at build time and are not streamed or downloaded.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Whisper Ball is suitable for general audiences, including children. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 7. Data Security

Because no data leaves your device, there is no transmission for us to secure. Locally stored progress data is protected by your device's standard application sandbox.

---

## 8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, the rights granted under privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and similar regulations — including access, correction, deletion, and portability — are satisfied by the app's design: there is no personal data on our side to access, correct, delete, or export.

To delete locally stored progress, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device's system settings.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy (for example, if we add an optional online leaderboard in a future version), we will update the "Last Updated" date above and post the change at the URL where this policy is hosted. Material changes that introduce data collection will be announced in the app's release notes.
